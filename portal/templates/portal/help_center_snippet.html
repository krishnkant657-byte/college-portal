Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay my semester fees?
You can pay your semester fees by navigating to the "Fees & Receipts" tab. We accept UPI, Net Banking, and Credit/Debit cards.
How is attendance calculated?
Attendance is calculated cumulatively across all subjects. A minimum of 75% is required to be eligible for the end-semester exams.
Can I change my registered email?
To change your registered email, please submit an "IT Issue" support ticket using the form on this page. Our admins will verify and update it for you.
How do I get my ID card?
Navigate to the "Identity Card" tab. Ensure your profile photo is uploaded. You can then download or print your digital ID card directly.
